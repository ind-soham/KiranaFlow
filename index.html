Having sales is not the same as having proof of sales.

  1. 01

    Money gets stuck

    Shop owners pay distributors today while customers may pay later on udhaar.

  2. 02

    No proof of business

    Sales often live in paper records or memory, making them difficult for formal lenders to verify.

  3. 03

    Working capital becomes expensive

    Without formal credit access, merchants may rely on expensive informal borrowing or reduce inventory.

The missing piece is proof, not money.

If daily business activity can become a reliable track record, a lending partner can evaluate the merchant with better information.

An honest path to working capital.

Designed around consent, transparency and responsible lending workflows.

  • No approval promises

    Loan approval is the lending partner's decision.

  • Consent first

    Data is shared with a lending partner only after merchant consent.

  • Minimum data

    We collect what the product needs — not contacts, photos, location tracking or personal messages.

  • Full cost

    The partner presents the full cost and repayment terms before the merchant accepts.

  • No loan money through KiranaFlow

    The lending partner sends funds directly to the merchant.
